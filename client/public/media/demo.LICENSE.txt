SHA-256 of demo.webm:
0e3b0cdacfd6d65a5e424eaca2988597f5f39e923a0a673985faa4daf115b231

Reproduction (FFmpeg with libvpx-vp9 and libopus):
ffmpeg -ss 30 -i BigBuckBunny_640x360.m4v -t 30 -map 0:v:0 -map 0:a:0 -vf "fps=24,scale=640:360:flags=lanczos,setsar=1" -c:v libvpx-vp9 -crf 36 -b:v 0 -pix_fmt yuv420p -row-mt 1 -cpu-used 4 -threads 2 -c:a libopus -b:a 64k -ac 2 -ar 48000 -map_metadata -1 demo.webm

Encoding output may differ across FFmpeg versions. Reproduction is optional:
the ready-to-use demo.webm is included in the repository.
